Click Clack is a writing studio for people who will be asked what AI did.

Named collaborators — Figments — propose, research, and comment. A separate, signed receipt records selected writing activity, including accepted AI suggestions and recorded pastes. You choose whether to download and share it to help explain your process. It can be independently checked, but it isn’t proof of who wrote the text.

## Does Click Clack add a watermark to my manuscript?

Click Clack doesn’t add a watermark to your manuscript. Text from a model or pasted from elsewhere may already contain watermark signals. The receipt is a separate file; it does not remove those signals.

Native paste recording is automatic, with [coverage gaps during shared editing](/export-signed-history#record-pasted-text). Downloading and sharing the receipt is optional.

This documentation is customer-facing. It is not an internal runbook. The job it has to do:

1. Help a **writer** export a signed history from Click Clack.
2. Help a **publisher, editor, or platform** check that file without a Click Clack account, and without trusting Click Clack’s website to tell the truth.

The second of those is the point. A receipt you can only view inside the product is a claim. A receipt a stranger can check on their own machine is a check.

## Who this is for

Writers who need to explain their process — novelists, journalists, and anyone else whose publisher, editor, or platform will ask what a machine contributed. The person you hand the file to is the second reader of these pages. They should be able to follow [Check a receipt](/check-a-receipt) without signing in.

## What you will not find here

Operator runbooks, design-system internals, and working notes stay in the product repository. They are not this site.

<Note>
  Verification is never colour alone. In Click Clack a signed contribution shows an icon and a label. On this site, a check is an exit status from the verifier, not a green badge in a screenshot.
</Note>
